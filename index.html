Upload. Plan. Build. Reset. Repeat.

How Waystone Forge Works

Here's the short version: You get your runes into the app - either by screenshotting your inventory or typing them in by hand - then review what got added before anything gets tracked. From there, you pick which waystones you want to build, tier by tier, and building is the one step that actually spends your runes. If you don't have the vita to actually build all the decks, no problem! Download a checklist so you can remember what you wanted.

Getting runes in

You've got two options here. Upload screenshots of your in-game inventory and let the app read them for you (there's a few tips above the upload button for getting the cleanest results) - or, if you'd rather skip screenshots altogether, Add a Rune Manually lets you type them in by hand instead. Either way, nothing actually gets added to your inventory until you've looked it over and confirmed it.

Your inventory

Everything you've confirmed shows up in Your Inventory, sorted by tier. If something ends up under the wrong tier, just use its Tier dropdown to move it - it'll merge into a matching stack if one's already there, rather than splitting into a duplicate.

Building a deck

Each tier gets its own tab - it'll turn green once there's at least one waystone you can actually build there, and stays red until then. Want to narrow things down first? Uncheck any Fortune you'd rather hold onto, or check off an Omen you'd rather not use, and the app will only count what's left. Not sure what one of them actually does? Hover its name for the full effect text.

Check off whichever waystones you're after and set how many of each you want. The suggested amounts keep adjusting as you go - so if one waystone you've already committed to eats into a rune type another one needs, that other one's suggested quantity updates right along with it.

Once you've picked what you want, hit Find Combinations and it'll show you every way you could actually build it, laid out as a set of options - there's always a "Don't build this" choice too, in case you change your mind. If you've got more than one named rune of a type that could cover what's needed, you'll see a change link next to the app's automatic pick (always the one you have the most of) - so you can swap it for a different one you'd rather use instead. Hover any tag you see there, too, for the full effect text on that specific rune - Fortune tags use [square brackets], Omen tags use <angle brackets>, so the shape alone tells you which is which, even without relying on color. Nothing gets deducted until you hit Build Selected, though - that's the only button that actually spends your runes.

When you're done

Once everything you've picked is actually built, you'll see a Download Build Checklist button show up. It's a standalone file you can open right on your phone and check off as you build each one in-game - it's even got its own Save a Copy of This Page and Discard This Checklist buttons. And if you ever want a clean slate, Reset will clear out your whole tracked inventory.

Troubleshooting Mode

Shows you the raw text the app actually read off each row. You'll already see this automatically for any row that needs a second look - this toggle just lets you see it for every row, in case you're curious or something's not reading right.

Colorblind Mode

Flip this on and you'll get a ✓ or ✗ on each tier tab, so you can ignore the green and red while still knowing whether you have build options or not.

1 Add runes from a screenshot

✓ Do

  • Start at the bottom of your rune list - scroll all the way down past them first, then work your way back up.
  • Scroll until the last rune you captured fully disappears off the top of the screen before the next shot.

✗ Don't

  • Leave gaps between screenshots - a missed rune won't show up anywhere, with no way to know it's gone.
  • Worry about overlap near the end of your list, where you can't scroll further - duplicates get flagged and unchecked automatically; just confirm the flag.

Upload several at once or one at a time - they pool into one review list below, where rows near an edge are unchecked by default in case they got cut off.
